# Product hero shot templates

For standalone product glamour shots (not UGC). Nano Banana 2 prompts.

## Base template

```
Hyperrealistic product photo: [PRODUCT] in [COLOR/MATERIAL],
[KEY DETAILS — 3 to 5 specifics],
[BACKGROUND],
[LIGHTING — direction + quality],
[DEPTH OF FIELD],
[ANGLE],
editorial e-commerce style,
[ASPECT RATIO].
```

## Style variants

### Clean e-commerce (the default)

```
Hyperrealistic product photo: [PRODUCT] in [COLOR],
[KEY DETAILS],
clean pure white seamless background,
studio softbox lighting from above and left casting soft natural shadow,
shallow depth of field,
3/4 profile angle,
editorial e-commerce style,
1:1 square aspect ratio.
```

Use for: listing thumbnails, reference images for Seedance, standalone hero shots.

### Moody editorial

```
Hyperrealistic product photo: [PRODUCT] in [COLOR],
[KEY DETAILS],
deep matte charcoal paper background with subtle texture,
single directional rim light from top-right carving the edge,
warm shadow on the left side, low fill,
shallow depth of field,
3/4 profile angle,
cinematic editorial style,
1:1 square aspect ratio.
```

Use for: launch announcements, premium brand positioning.

### Flat lay

```
Hyperrealistic top-down flat lay photograph:
[PRODUCT] centered, surrounded by [COMPLEMENTARY OBJECTS — coffee, keys, book],
warm natural light from the right,
textured wood surface,
slight shadow depth,
editorial lifestyle style,
1:1 square aspect ratio.
```

Use for: lifestyle positioning, social feed pacing between pure product shots.

### Lifestyle environment

```
Hyperrealistic product photograph:
[PRODUCT] placed on [SURFACE — kitchen counter, desk, bedside table]
in a [ENVIRONMENT — minimal home interior, warm office, sunlit bedroom],
natural window light from the left casting soft morning shadow,
shallow depth of field focused on product,
slight environmental context visible but not competing,
3/4 angle,
editorial lifestyle style,
4:5 aspect ratio.
```

Use for: ads targeting a specific context ("morning routine", "office ready", "evening unwind").

## Tips

- **Always say "hyperrealistic"** — Nano Banana 2 drifts stylized without this
- **Always name a light direction** — "studio lighting" alone is too vague
- **Default to 1:1** for Seedance reference (easier to compose into any aspect later)
- **3–5 detail callouts, not more** — too many and the model starts averaging
- **Avoid text on the product** — reliability drops fast beyond 3 letters. Use symbols or composite logo in post
- **For variation, regenerate rather than retouch** — faster and cleaner results
